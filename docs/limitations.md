# Limitations

SayShape draws everything itself, so it gives up what the browser normally provides for free. Today it does **not** have:

- **Forms beyond the basics.** `input` takes several lines (`lines=`) and says what's wrong once a field has been left (`required=`, `kind=email`, `kind=number`, or its own `error=`), but there's no form element that checks every field at once on submit, no rich text editing, and no pattern or length checks beyond `error=` and `max=`. A submit button can be held back with `disabled={…}`.
- **Selecting text beyond the basics.** Text in `text`, `stat`, `table`, `details`, `callout`, `badge` and list items can be selected with a mouse or pen (drag, double-click for a word, triple-click for a block, Shift+click to extend), copied and found with Ctrl/Cmd+F (see [engine.md](engine.md)). Text inside something pressable keeps the plain click: no word selecting on double-click there. Long-press selection on touch screens is built but off by default until it has been checked on real phones (`selectTouch: true`). Chart labels, scenes, slides and text in other elements aren't covered, and `copyText()` still gives the whole screen when nothing is selected.
- **Toasts or sheets.** `tip=` on any element shows a short tooltip. Popups serve one element's own panel (a select's list, a date picker's calendar, a menu's actions; a menu has no submenus or separators), and `dialog` is the one overlay: one open at a time, centred, over a dimmed screen. There are no toasts or sheets that slide in from an edge. `scroll` scrolls up and down only, at one of three heights, and lays out everything inside it (no virtualization), so very long lists should still be paged.
- **Dates and times beyond the basics.** `date-picker` picks a calendar day (or a range with `range=true`), with no times. `schedule` lays out times as `HH:MM` text and dates as `YYYY-MM-DD`, but has no time zones, recurring events, blocks past midnight, month view, or dragging blocks to move them. Names are in US English with weeks starting on Sunday.
- **Selects beyond the basics.** At most 200 options, one choice or several (`multiple=true`), with no typing to search, option groups, or options showing anything but text.
- **Tables beyond the basics.** `table` shows at most 200 rows and 64 columns. A table too wide for its space scrolls sideways, but long tables don't scroll up and down, and there are no sticky headers, editable cells, multi-row selection, nested elements in cells, or resizing and reordering columns.
- **Charts beyond the basics.** `chart` draws bars (up or across, `kind=hbar`, or stacked), lines or areas of up to 6 series on one shared scale (with a legend), or a pie or donut of one field (at most 6 slices; smaller ones are summed into Other), from at most 200 items with labels evenly spaced in list order. Horizontal bars stop at 1200 px of rows and ask for `take()`. A stat's sparkline takes a list of numbers; `pluck()` takes one from a list of records. `kind=scatter` has a numeric x-axis; the other kinds space their items evenly in list order. No series from different lists, zoom, time axes, or pressing a bar.
- **Slides beyond the basics.** A `slide` is laid out at 960 by 540 pixels and drawn scaled to its width, always 16:9. Content too tall for it shrinks, to 60% at the least, and past that is cut off at the bottom with a warning to split the slide in two (the warning names the slide and fires once). A `table` or `list` shows about five rows, so show the top few with `take()` and `sort()`. A title fits two lines (the opening slide's three), a subtitle two, and a `layout=agenda` slide lists at most six lines, the last "and N more" (`MAX_AGENDA`). Only the slide shown is laid out, the dots under a deck are left out when too many to fit between the arrows (the arrows and keys still work), and a deck has no speaker view, notes, per-slide backgrounds or transitions other than a fade. The MCP view saves a deck as a PDF or a PowerPoint file of pictures, at most 60 slides, that can't be edited.
- **A look beyond a few roles.** A look line (`look mood=… type=… shape=… density=…`) picks from 2 or 3 named options per prop, laid over the app's theme. It can't set colors (they come only from the app's theme and brand colors), name a font (the faces are fixed stacks ending in system faces; the engine never loads a font, so a face the app hasn't loaded falls back, to Georgia for the serif stacks), set sizes, or change one element's look: it is one choice for the whole interface. Headings take the display face only in `text style=title` and `display` (not card titles, stat figures or slide headings), and inside slides only `mood` and the faces apply. Image treatments and page elements (hero, section, band) are not built yet.
- **Motion beyond entrances.** `enter=fade|rise|scale` and `stagger=true` play once, when an element first comes into sight; there are no exits, no motion on change, no scroll-linked effects, and a spec can't set durations, delays or distances. An element counts as in sight from the page's scroll position and the scrolling element around it, not an ancestor of the canvas that clips it with its own overflow; and in a host that grows the canvas to fit its content with nothing to scroll (the MCP view in a chat), everything is in sight at once. While a stream is restarted with `keepState`, an element whose line and type match one already shown doesn't play again. Reduced motion turns every entrance off, even with `animate: true`.
- **Right-to-left or complex-script text shaping** beyond what the canvas does on its own.
- **Behavior beyond the basics** (experimental, off by default). Rules count runs, not clock time: they pause while the page is hidden or the screen is out of sight and don't catch up, so a countdown in a hidden tab pauses. They can't loop over a list's items (simulations of many things move them by formulas of a counter), call the app, or open a dialog, and the end of a rule isn't announced to screen readers. Positions jump from tick to tick, with no smoothing between. Dragging works only on scene shapes with one `x` and `y` (put a `line`, `path` or `polygon` in a `group`), one finger at a time, with no drop targets. Wall-clock time for timers is the first thing to revisit.

Accessibility is covered by a hidden mirror of the canvas (see [Accessibility](engine.md#accessibility)); check it with assistive technology before relying on it where conformance is required.
